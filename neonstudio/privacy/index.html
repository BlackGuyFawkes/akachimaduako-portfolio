Introduction

neonstudio (Akachi Maduako) publishes free Android games. We designed these games to be playable fully offline and to avoid collecting personal information. This policy explains the data practices that apply across the neonstudio game catalogue.

This policy also applies to future games published by neonstudio unless a future app provides a separate privacy policy.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or share personal data.

The games do not require accounts or login information. They do not use analytics, advertising, third-party SDKs, or network requests. The games are playable fully offline.

How Data Is Stored

The games may store limited gameplay information locally on your device, such as best scores and settings. This information is stored using local app storage through shared_preferences.

This local gameplay data never leaves your device. It is not sent to neonstudio or any third party. The locally stored data is removed when the app is uninstalled.

Data Sharing

There is no personal-data sharing because the games do not collect or transmit personal data. neonstudio does not sell, rent, disclose, or transfer personal data from these games to advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Children's Privacy

The games are not directed at children under 13. neonstudio does not knowingly collect personal data from children or from any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If the data practices of the games change, or if this policy otherwise needs to be updated, the revised policy will be posted at this website with an updated effective date. The current version posted here is the policy that applies to the covered games.
