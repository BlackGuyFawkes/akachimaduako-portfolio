Manufacturing OT

GridWatch Modbus detection

SuricataZeekSplunkModbus

Ran a 169-packet synthetic Modbus capture through Suricata and Zeek, then indexed the alerts in Splunk. Suricata raised 122 alerts across six signatures, led by unauthorized writes (44), coil writes (43) and write floods (28). Zeek extracted four TCP connections. Offline analysis only; no industrial equipment was connected.

Ransomware tabletop for a manufacturer

CISA CTEPIncident responseOT safety

Designed a 60 to 75 minute discussion exercise for a fictional Ontario auto-parts plant, adapted from CISA Critical Manufacturing materials. Injects move from file changes on an engineering workstation to SMB activity toward OT, vendor remote access and recovery uncertainty. Facilitator prompts cover incident authority, safety before isolation, PLC integrity and manual operations. This is the exercise plan; a conducted session is not claimed.
