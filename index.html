About me

I investigate security incidents and test systems the way an attacker would.

I work on both sides of security. On the defensive side I triage alerts, correlate endpoint, identity, email and network telemetry, and write up what the evidence does and does not support. On the offensive side I scope, exploit and document web, API and network weaknesses with clear remediation. My recent work focuses on banking SOC detections such as card-testing fraud and identity attacks.

I hold CompTIA Security+ and Fortinet NSE 1 to 3, and I bring monitoring and escalation experience from a security operations centre. I am based in Toronto and open to SOC, threat detection, fraud monitoring and penetration testing roles in the GTA or remote across Canada.
